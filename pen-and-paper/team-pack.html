The Great Trophy Heist

Breaking newsNamma Stadium, Bengaluru

The golden trophy has VANISHED! Tonight is the big final: the Bengaluru Blasters vs the Mumbai Mavericks. Fifty thousand fans are on their way, but the trophy is gone. A note was left behind:

"Catch me if you can.
The Googly Ghost."

You are the Stadium Detectives!

  1. Draw the suspects. You need to recognise them!
  2. Crack 6 clues. Do them in any order.
  3. Each clue gives a secret letter on your Decoder Sheet.
  4. Six letters make a word. That word is where the trophy is hidden.
  5. Tell your facilitator the word to find the trophy!

Clue cards 1-3
Clue 1Namma Metro

Metro Mystery

CCTV shows the Googly Ghost escaping on the Metro with the trophy bag. Follow the ghost's journey!

The ghost's voice note: "I got on at Trinity and rode 3 stops towards Majestic. Then I realised I forgot my chai, so I went 1 stop back. I got off there."

Your task: Which station did the ghost get off at? Put your finger on the map and count each stop.

Clue 2Namma Tiffin Room

Dosa Receipt

The ghost stopped for a snack and left this crumpled receipt.

MenuPrice
Masala Dosa₹40
Filter Coffee₹20
Medu Vada₹30

The ghost ordered: 2 Masala Dosas, 3 Filter Coffees and 1 Medu Vada.
Coupon in the ghost's pocket: "₹20 OFF the final bill!"

Your task: How many rupees did the ghost really pay?

Clue cards 4-6
Clue 4The scorecard

Over Maths

The vault under the stadium opens with a PIN. The ghost hid the rule inside the scorecard of the last over (6 balls).

Ball 1Ball 2Ball 3Ball 4Ball 5Ball 6
46W126

W means a wicket: no runs. Extras: 1 wide (adds 1 run to the team total).

PIN rule: "Write the total runs of the over, including extras. Then write how many sixes were hit. Put them together."

Your task: What is the vault PIN?

Clue 5CCTV

Who Did It?

Four people were near the trophy room. The CCTV is blurry but it shows clues about the thief (the real person inside the ghost costume).

  1. The thief was wearing a cap.
  2. The thief was not wearing glasses.
  3. The thief reached the top shelf easily, so they are tall.

Use YOUR drawings! Put the 4 suspect cards on the table: Coach Ramesh, Bisi the Bear, Murthy Anna and Neha. Turn over anyone who does not match a clue.

Your task: Who is the thief?

Clue 6Traffic camera

Silk Board Jam

The ghost's getaway scooter is stuck at the Silk Board junction! A traffic camera counted how many cars passed each minute.

361224?

Look at how the number of cars changes from one minute to the next. Find the pattern!

Your task: How many cars will pass in minute 5?
